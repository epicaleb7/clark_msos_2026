C1_ThreeZeros.m and C1_Matching.m were NOT run over QQalal = QQal(rt3).  (30 Oct 2026)
=====================================================================================

For their output, see the QQal folder:
    ../by_cases_caleb_style/C1_ThreeZeros_output.txt
    ../by_cases_caleb_style/C1_Matching_output.txt
The results over QQalal are the same: same components, same OC vectors, same orbits.

Why they weren't run
--------------------
Groebner bases over the relative field ext<QQal | xx^2 - 3> are very slow in Magma
(about 14 minutes for one two-zero case), and nothing new can come out of these two files.

Why nothing changes (how this was verified)
-------------------------------------------
If no component splits further when sqrt3 is added, then the primes over QQalal are exactly
the QQal primes, the group A acts on them in the same way, and so ReduceByGInQQalal would give
exactly the same OC vectors and representatives as ReduceByGInQQal.

To check that nothing splits, CheckNothingSplitsOverQQalal.m counts the components over
QQ(zeta8, sqrt3), built as CyclotomicField(24) (the same field as QQal(rt3), but fast in Magma),
without the slow orbit reduction. Output: CheckNothingSplitsOverQQalal_output.txt.

    case        components over QQal    components over QQ(zeta8, sqrt3)
    a=b                 2                        2
    a=c                 8                        8
    a=e                32                       32
    a=f                32                       32
    a=i                32                       32
    b=d                32                       32
    b=e                 8                        8
    b=h                 8                        8
    {b,d,i}            32                       32
    {b,e,h}            32                       32
    {a,e,i}            32                       32
    Sing(X)           256                      256

All the same, so nothing splits, and the QQal outputs are also the QQalal outputs.
(The 13 empty triples and the 23 empty four-zero cases are empty over every field:
emptiness is decided by the radical, which doesn't depend on the field.)

Note: the 27 Sep "All absolutely irreducible" check is NOT used as evidence here: the old
CheckAllAbsIrred had a bug (it broke out of the loop before recording a failure), so it could
print "All absolutely irreducible" even when that was false.
